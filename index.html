01 / Research

Questions & interests

I am interested in the interplay between symmetry, geometry, and non-linear dynamics in field theory.

Field theory & duality

Chiral p-form gauge theories, electromagnetic duality, and non-linear deformations, including T T-bar and root-T T-bar flows.

Geometry & integrability

Differential geometry, Poisson structures, Hamiltonian systems, and integrable hierarchies.

Master’s thesis · 2026

Non-linear p-form gauge theories and their deformations

My thesis studied deformations of chiral p-form theories, with a focus on the relation between six- and ten-dimensional constructions.

Supervisors: Prof. Kurt Lechner and Prof. Dmitri Sorokin · University of Padua
